Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2024

Last Updated: November 17, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to RATIX ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and game.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Player Name: Optional display name for gameplay
  • Game Data: High scores, game preferences, and match history
  • Account Information: If you create an account (email via Firebase Authentication)
  • Payment Information: If you purchase premium features (processed securely by Stripe)

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Game statistics, session duration, features used
  • Device Information: Browser type, device type, operating system
  • IP Address: For security and analytics purposes
  • Cookies: For functionality and advertising (see Cookie Policy below)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the RATIX game experience
  • Save your progress and high scores
  • Enable multiplayer functionality
  • Process payments for premium features
  • Display relevant advertisements (via Google AdSense)
  • Analyze usage patterns and optimize performance
  • Communicate important updates or changes
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services, which employ industry-standard encryption and security measures. We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information.

  • Data Encryption: All data transmitted is encrypted using SSL/TLS
  • Secure Authentication: Firebase Authentication with industry-standard security
  • Payment Security: We never store payment card details (handled by Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant provider)

  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • Privacy Policy: https://stripe.com/privacy

5.4 Google Analytics

  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for game functionality and user authentication
  • Analytics Cookies: To understand how users interact with our game
  • Advertising Cookies: To display relevant ads via Google AdSense

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect game functionality.

7. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

8. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at: skobelgames@gmail.com

9. Children's Privacy

RATIX is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

10. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Game Data: Retained for service functionality
  • Analytics Data: Retained for 26 months (Google Analytics default)
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years for tax compliance

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place when transferring data internationally, in compliance with GDPR and other applicable laws.
